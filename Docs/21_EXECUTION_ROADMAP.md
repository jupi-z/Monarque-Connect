# 21 — Roadmap d'exécution

## Phase 0 — Fondation
Repo, Node/TS/Nest, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Docker, config, health, OpenAPI validation, CI.

## Phase 1 — Identity & tenancy
Auth, users, organizations, memberships, invitations, RBAC, tenant isolation.

## Phase 2 — Référentiels
Business parties + catalog + warehouses.

## Phase 3 — CRM
Leads + opportunities.

## Phase 4 — Sales 1
Quotes.

## Phase 5 — Sales 2
Orders.

## Phase 6 — Billing
Invoices + payment records.

## Phase 7 — Operations
Expenses + inventory.

## Phase 8 — Network
Public profiles + discovery + connections.

## Phase 9 — Messaging & documents
Conversations, messages, documents/media.

## Phase 10 — Platform control plane
Administration Monarque Group.

## Phase 11 — Hardening/release
Security, concurrency, migration, perf, backup/restore, staging, release.

### Règle
Une phase ne modifie pas les phases précédentes sans migration et tests de non-régression.
